# Competitive landscape

| Reference | Relevant strength | SenseLab implication | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| [PhET accessibility](https://phet.colorado.edu/en/accessibility) | Alternative input and descriptions | Treat simulation state as semantic data | PhET has a much larger mature library |
| [Desmos accessibility](https://www.desmos.com/accessibility) | Audio trace and accessible mathematics | Offer table, graph and sound from one dataset | SenseLab is not a general calculator |
| [MAIDR](https://maidr.ai/) | Multimodal statistical visualization | Keep modalities synchronized | Its focus differs from full inquiry orchestration |
| [CAST UDL](https://udlguidelines.cast.org/) | Multiple means of representation and expression | Preserve learner choice and reasoning | UDL is a framework, not a test |
| [Perkins](https://www.perkins.org/) and [APH](https://www.aph.org/) | Accessible education resources | Prioritize independence and educator validation | Resources do not replace participant research |

SenseLab's narrow differentiation is the integration of nonvisual variable control, deterministic simulation, multimodal data exploration, a structured notebook and reasoning assessment in one inquiry loop. It does not claim to invent accessible graphing or replace physical laboratories.
